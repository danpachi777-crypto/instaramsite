# Native scene recordings

The four WebM clips and WebP posters are browser recordings of this repository's live Three.js scene. They are not generated photographs or recordings of the qu.ai website.

The posters are captured at 2880 × 1800. The videos are recorded at 1440 × 810, 30 frames per second, using the browser's canvas capture stream. The scene uses the model and texture provenance recorded in `../world/SOURCES.json` and `../animals/SOURCES.md`.
